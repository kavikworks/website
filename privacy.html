Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Kavik Works, operated by Robot Arms LLC, Denver, CO, wrote this policy for kavikworks.com and the $149 one-time written Workflow Blueprint. The blueprint is a written plan for one intake or follow-up workflow. We prepare it with AI assistance and review it before delivery.

There is no Kavik Works account. We do not ask for access to your inbox or other systems.

Information you send by email

If you write to josh@kavikworks.com, we receive that email. It may include your email address, your name, your business, and the workflow you describe. We use it to check whether the workflow fits, to prepare and review the Workflow Blueprint, and to handle delivery, a clarification, or a refund.

Describe the process in general terms. Do not send passwords, card numbers, customer records, or other sensitive personal information.

The free workflow check

Answers entered in the workflow check stay in the page until you choose to send them with your own email application. The check does not submit those answers to a server or store them in your browser.

Campaign labels

A link may contain campaign labels identifying a marketing source and post. We carry these labels through links within this website and can include them in an email draft to understand which material led to an inquiry. They do not identify an individual visitor. The workflow check lets you leave them out, and you can remove them from any draft before sending. This feature uses no cookies, browser storage, tracking pixels, or background event collection.

Payment

Payment is collected on a Stripe-hosted checkout page, not in an email and not in a form on this website. Stripe handles payment card data and the billing details it asks for. We do not receive or store your full card number or security code. Stripe may share the details we need to recognize the order, such as the amount, a receipt, and the email address you give Stripe.

What this site does not collect

We do not set cookies. We do not run analytics, advertising pixels, or other tracking. We do not sell personal information.

AI assistance

An AI tool may process the workflow description you send while we draft the written blueprint. A person reviews the draft against your brief before delivery. We do not connect that tool to your accounts or inbox. Send only a business-level description you are comfortable having a drafting tool process.

Who else handles information

  • Stripe, when you pay, for checkout and refunds.
  • The email service that receives mail to josh@kavikworks.com.
  • Cloudflare, which hosts this static website.

We may also share information when the law requires it, or to respond to a security, fraud, or payment dispute.

How long we keep it

We keep the emails and purchase records needed to deliver the blueprint, cover the clarification and refund window, and meet ordinary bookkeeping needs. You can ask us to delete a workflow description when we no longer need it for those purposes. A payment record may need to stay while bookkeeping or a dispute requires it.

Your requests

Email josh@kavikworks.com to ask what we have, to correct it, or to ask us to delete it. What the law requires can depend on where you live.

Children

This is a business service. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and change the date above.

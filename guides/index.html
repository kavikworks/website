  • How to stop missing calls and leads at a small HVAC or plumbing shop

    For searches about missed calls while you are on a job: one list, two callback windows, and a voicemail that collects the work.

  • A simple job intake and scheduling workflow without new software

    For a spreadsheet or notebook that shows the request, the owner, and the window. Fits HVAC, plumbing, electrical, cleaning, landscaping, and handyman work.

  • How to follow up on estimates so a quote does not go cold

    For quotes you already sent: a status list, an example contact rhythm you can change, and three short messages that do not invent urgency.

  • How to handle maintenance requests for a small property portfolio

    For owners and small managers: log the repair, separate safety issues, hand a vendor one message, and close the loop with the resident.

  • How to schedule recurring cleaning clients without losing the route

    For a weekly or biweekly cleaning route: client cards, a skip rule, and a day-before confirmation.

  • How to reduce no-shows for home service appointments

    For crews who arrive to an empty house: define a no-show, confirm access when you book, and send a reminder you will actually send.

  • What to do with after-hours calls at a small electrical or handyman business

    For nights and weekends: a closed-hours greeting, a morning sweep, and a plain split between safety calls and ordinary quotes.

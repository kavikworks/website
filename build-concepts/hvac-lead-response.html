Practical workflow planning

Quote-request routing for home service businesses

Plan the handoff from a normal service inquiry to an office or estimating owner.

Planning example. This illustrates a possible workflow, not a deployed customer system or measured result.

The handoff to define

Capture the service location, requested work, contact method, and timing. Separate a request for a quote from an existing appointment. The office confirms service area and missing details before assigning an estimator.

Keep the scope clear

Do not promise availability, prices, emergency response, or dispatch through an unverified automated reply.

A small test

Walk through a complete inquiry, a missing-detail case, a duplicate, a reply, an opt-out, and a failed tool connection. Record the owner and next action for each case. Nothing should disappear because an integration is unavailable.

What to measure

Use your own request volume, manual handling time, time to a useful reply, unassigned requests, and known outcomes. No performance results are claimed for this example.

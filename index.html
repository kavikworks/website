Before you start

A few useful answers.

Will this run an automation for me?

The $149 blueprint is a written plan. It includes routing rules, templates, setup steps, and a test checklist. It does not include installed software, live inbox access, or ongoing monitoring. If implementation looks worthwhile, we can assess a separate fixed scope.

Do I need a call or a new subscription?

No call is required for a blueprint. We work by email and start with your existing tools. If a tool would cost extra, that cost is identified in the plan for you to decide.

Who is this most useful for?

Small service businesses and property teams with a repeatable quote, leasing, or administrative intake process. A workflow involving medical, legal, financial, or tenant-screening decisions, or emergency dispatch, is outside this offer.

What if the answer is a simpler manual process?

That belongs in the plan. A shared inbox rule, a clearer template, or an assigned owner can be more useful than an AI integration. The aim is a process your team can maintain.

Can you promise more sales or time saved?

No. We define a baseline and a small test so you can measure the result in your own business. The examples on this site illustrate workflows; they are not customer case studies.

What happens after I request a blueprint?

Email us and we confirm fit and delivery timing before payment. If you already know the workflow, you can pay on the Stripe checkout page and email the details afterward.

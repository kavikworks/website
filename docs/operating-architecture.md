Before requesting payment, inventory complete one-workflow intake, paid work and due dates, open payable invitations and reserved commitments, production/revision/correction effort, independent agent QA availability, actual private delivery/readback/recipient-access capability, tax/payment readiness, and contingency. Multiple payable invitations can overbook capacity before becoming paid work. Prioritize paid deadlines, corrections, and revisions; pause further intake before foreseeable overdue work, failed QA, tool/access loss, material quality problems, or refund issues. Promise the default five business days only when this inventory supports it. Unknown capacity cannot justify a commitment, and this check does not change price, terms, or scope.

## Measurement and decision gates

Owner Brief reconciles costs, collected revenue, refunds and fees when available, delivery quality, revisions, actual founder minutes, capacity, material errors, and blockers. Report raw counts, date range or cohort, source, and coverage before rates. Unknown values remain `UNKNOWN`; they are never treated as zero.

Give each cohort at least seven calendar days of response opportunity before judging the tested factor. Count actual attempts, sends, confirmed delivery versus unknown delivery, human replies, qualified inquiries, opt-outs, bounces, and immutable paid orders separately. If delivery receipts are unavailable, use a clearly labeled sent-recipient fallback; never call those recipients delivered. Opens and mailto clicks are not buyer outcomes. Low exposure or imbalance is inconclusive. Compare outcomes, known attributable cost, contribution, founder minutes, delivery errors, and revisions from the same cohort. A single order does not establish a winning channel. A volume increase requires the documented readiness and response evidence review above; a favorable copy result alone cannot clear provider or fulfillment gates.

Owner Brief records `KEEP`, `REVISE`, `PAUSE`, or `INCONCLUSIVE`, the evidence, one next change, an owner, and a review date. Growth Studio reads that decision before production. Do not scale beyond the capacity, quality, payment, or evidence gates.

## Objective and product

The operating objective is useful contribution with minimal founder time. The jobs should pursue a profitable business while keeping evidence honest, preserving customer obligations, and escalating decisions that exceed the standing policy. The initial offer is a fixed, asynchronous test of this objective; it is not established product-market fit.

The Workflow Blueprint remains a $149 USD one-time written deliverable for one quote-request, leasing-inquiry, or shared administrative-inbox workflow. It includes a process map, capture fields, routing rules, three editable templates, setup steps, tests, and one clarification revision within seven days of delivery. It excludes installed software, ongoing monitoring, emergency dispatch, regulated advice, and unlimited support. Confirm scope, applicable tax, payment method, and delivery timing from current private state before requesting or accepting payment. Any price, public scope, or terms change requires a separate concrete owner decision plus matching live site, checkout, and ledger evidence.

Start at two new paid orders per week and two in progress. Promise five business days only when capacity, tools, and source material are ready. After three on-time paid, QA-passed, recipient-access-verified deliveries, no unresolved material defects or refund disputes, and a sustainable workload, Owner Brief may authorize a measured increase to five new orders per week and three in progress. That decision does not change the public price, terms, or scope.

## Acquisition and sender boundaries

The acquisition loop is intentionally bounded while evidence is gathered. Business Desk is the only job allowed to send one-to-one business mail. It may run an initial targeted US B2B sequence under these limits:

- At most five new recipients per weekday and 25 per calendar week.
- One follow-up per recipient, at least seven calendar days after the initial message; two touches total.
- Public, verified business email and the approved sender identity only.
- No guessed addresses, bought lists, personal-profile spam, impersonation, old-sequence restart, or second sender.
- A reply, opt-out, bounce, manual message, existing draft, or relevant domain suppression stops cold sequencing for that recipient or domain as applicable. Existing customer replies continue through the service path.
- Verify the actual sender, full thread, recipient, domain suppression, address and unsubscribe requirements, and applicable commercial-message rules from current private state before sending.
- Stop all new outbound when a complaint or provider warning occurs, or when two hard bounces appear in the last 25 outbound attempts. Preserve inbound and existing-customer handling, record the blocker, investigate the cause, and require an actual correction plus verified safe restoration before resuming.

Growth Studio owns one hypothesis, audience, offer, channel, and observation window at a time. It selects one experiment per run and fills only a small execution queue. It may publish up to three organic business posts per week when an owned business channel is connected and verified. If that channel is unavailable, it uses a targeted-email or website fallback and records the blocker; it does not wait indefinitely for a social scheduler or produce an unbounded draft backlog.

Paid campaigns, new paid tools, and purchases stay off until a separate private decision verifies payment access, budget, conversion evidence, and terms. Card placeholder information never belongs in this repository.

## Measurement and decision gates

Owner Brief reconciles costs, collected revenue, refunds and fees when available, delivery quality, revisions, actual founder minutes, capacity, material errors, and blockers. Report raw counts, date range or cohort, source, and coverage before rates. Unknown values remain `UNKNOWN`; they are never treated as zero.

Give an acquisition experiment at least seven days and evidence of 20–40 unique delivered recipients where delivery is verified before a first decision. Count attempted, sent, delivered, and unknown separately. If delivery receipts are unavailable, use a clearly labeled sent-recipient fallback; never call those recipients delivered or fabricate unknown delivery. Low exposure is inconclusive. Compare qualified inquiries, verified payments, known attributable cost, contribution, founder minutes, delivery errors, and revisions from the same cohort. A single order does not establish a winning channel.

Owner Brief records `KEEP`, `REVISE`, `PAUSE`, or `INCONCLUSIVE`, the evidence, one next change, an owner, and a review date. Growth Studio reads that decision before production. Do not scale beyond the capacity, quality, payment, or evidence gates.

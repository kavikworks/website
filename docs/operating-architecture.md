# Low-maintenance operations

The operating objective is useful contribution with minimal founder time. The initial founder-time budget is 30 minutes per week after setup; actual time must be recorded before that target can be claimed. A fixed, asynchronous offer is a test of this objective, not established product-market fit.

## Product and capacity

Workflow Blueprint: $149 USD once, one workflow, a map, capture fields, routing rules, three templates, setup steps, test checklist, and one clarification revision within seven days of delivery. Work by email. Confirm scope and delivery date before any payment request. Do not promise installed software or ongoing monitoring.

Start at two new paid orders per week and two in progress. The first three paid deliveries need founder quality review, budgeted at 15 minutes each. Increasing volume or allowing unattended paid delivery requires a measured result and an explicit decision recorded in the private operations board. Preserve any existing signed customer obligations.

## Three jobs

| Job | Ownership | Normal output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business Desk | Business inbox, cases, routine inbound replies, delivery preparation, suppression | Updated private board; exceptional alerts only |
| Growth Studio | One bounded website or distribution improvement and its experiment | Evidence, change, validation, and observation window |
| Owner Brief | Profit, known costs, founder time, delivery quality, incidents, next decision | One concise weekly owner report |

Business Desk runs on working mornings and afternoons. Growth Studio runs weekly. Owner Brief runs weekly and absorbs the monthly visibility check. Their actual schedules live in the personal cloud scheduler. This repository does not create a second scheduler. Personal automations are separate.

## Records and truth

The private Notion Operations page is the start point. Its board contains gates, cases, action intents, errors, and experiments. Task prompts carry exact private page references; they are intentionally absent from this public repository. Gmail proves actual mail state. Existing Google Drive holds private customer deliverables. The old Outreach Master is historical suppression evidence, not a second active CRM. The wiki describes earlier positioning; current operational decisions take precedence.

Each case uses a Gmail thread ID. Each side effect uses a key built from thread ID, inbound message ID, and action type. Write PLANNED before acting, CONFIRMED only after verified completion, and UNKNOWN on uncertain completion. Do not replay unresolved actions. Reconcile the external system first.

Finish pagination or persist the continuation before advancing a scan cursor. Use overlapping scans and immutable IDs. If Notion, sender verification, or full-thread retrieval fails, send nothing. Keep the failure in one visible record rather than creating repeated alerts. Do not copy entire private messages into the repository.

## Acquisition and feedback

The primary path is a free public sample and workflow check, followed by an optional email request for the blueprint. Cold-email generation and legacy follow-up sending are stopped during migration. Do not introduce a second sender while vendor and local jobs remain unverified. A vendor-reported LinkedIn response is evidence to inspect, not a paid sale or permission to expand campaigns.

Measure qualified requests, verified payments, known attributable cost, contribution, actual founder minutes, material errors, and revisions. Mark missing metrics UNKNOWN. Report cost subtotals with coverage. Never treat unknown revenue or time as zero.

Review the first offer experiment after four weeks or ten qualified inquiries. Small samples are inconclusive. Change one variable at a time. If founder work exceeds the budget in two successive weeks, lower acceptance or simplify delivery before growing demand. Do not add new paid tools, ad spend, or schedules without a concrete owner decision.

## Site and intake

The static workflow check produces a generic starter template locally. It offers an encoded mailto link and a copy fallback. It makes no background POST, stores no answers, and never displays a delivery confirmation. The visitor must send the email. This is an intentional reliable bridge while an approved payment and intake service remains unverified.

Keep the established hosting and URLs. Retired concepts remain explicit noindex pages rather than unsupported industry service claims. Buyer guides and the sample stay indexable. Search or LLM citation improvements require observation; robots.txt and llms.txt are not performance evidence.

Legacy Apps Script and Cloud Function/VPS paths are not used by the current intake and have not been remotely disabled by this repository change. Do not claim otherwise. Before decommissioning any service, establish its live deployment, dependencies, signed client obligations, and billing effects.

## Publication

Run the site checks, inspect the diff, create a PR, and merge with ordinary repository protections. Verify the intended content on the public domain separately. If hosting has not deployed it, record that state and prepare the deployment handoff. Do not create an alternate hosting project or bypass protections.

All business changes remain bounded by the owner's mandate and actual available capabilities. Bank verification, signed commitments, sensitive customer decisions, new prices, and new spending are not routine agent optimizations.
